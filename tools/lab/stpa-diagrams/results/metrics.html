    STPA diagram lab: measured results¶

    Living document, last changed 2026-10-16 at 16a87d45.

    Generated by report.py from metrics.json; lower is better except the loop columns.

    column meaning
    levels node pairs drawn out of hierarchy order (higher level not above lower)
    columns same-level pairs drawn out of left-to-right lane order
    ctl left loop pairs whose control path runs left of its feedback path
    distinct loop pairs whose control and feedback paths stay more than 6 px apart
    cross edge crossings away from nodes
    overlap edge pairs drawn on top of each other
    through edges passing through a node they do not connect
    groups outside nodes inside a group's hull
    labels text runs overlapping another text run (more than 15% of the smaller)
    moved mean / max node displacement after adding one feedback edge, as a fraction of the drawing
