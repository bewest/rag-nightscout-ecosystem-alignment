    R24's seven findings¶

    1. Page loads: 21 differences, all BF-135's intended change. Every difference is AndroidAPS Temp Basals (aaps-tb-157, -205, -253, …) that arm A (15.0.8) sends to a newly opened page and arm B does not. A scratch check against the ledger (pageload-softdel.js) covered the raw page loads:

    • 23 raw differences (21 after the analyser's 10 s rule), each an A-only record whose v3 soft DELETE had been acknowledged more than 10 s earlier.
    • Arm A sends such records from tick 611, the first page load after the server restart at +35 min, to the end. Arm B never sends one.
    • Before the restart, neither arm sent one: 15.0.8's in-memory copy dropped the record at the delete, and its reload from MongoDB after the restart brought it back.
    • In R23, which had no restart, the same check finds none on either arm.

    This is BF-135 as the release notes describe it: on 15.0.8 a record AndroidAPS deleted comes back into COB, IOB and the page's data after a reload. The analyser has no expected-difference rule for page loads, and its "deleted records shown" check counts hard deletes only. Both are harness limits, recorded here and not changed in this run.

    2. nightguard.properties: cob.lastCarbs differs (5 signatures, 11 operations), after the server restart only. Both arms alternate, from one reload to the next, between two older carb entries that were edited through v1 (carb-549, carb-585, and once carb-621), and the two arms are out of phase. The key-set comparison sees it only when one of the two entries carries insulin: null and the other has no insulin key. Before the restart (tick 282), both arms reported the same entry. This is the BF-133 mechanism, which is live on 15.0.8 too: lib/plugins/cob.js takes the last treatment with carbs in array order. Neither arm is stable, so it is not a 15.0.9 regression. It did not appear in run 017's R18 with the same shape. Why the two arms' orders differ after the restart was not traced; run 019 should look for it again.

    3. nightguard.properties: bgnow present only on B, once (tick 282, before any disturbance). Arm A's bgnow was empty at the first ask and again 3 s later, right after a reading was acknowledged on both arms. It happened once, on the 15.0.8 arm, and was not traced.
