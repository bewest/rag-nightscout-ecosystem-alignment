    What changed¶

    Normal operational verification now drains owned clinical work before invalidating its admitted backend. Tickets remain queued, and a changed admission epoch causes a current full acquisition rather than discarding a newer model. Cold TIME work acquires retained state before evaluation; warm TIME retains the no-input-reload path. Background API initialization runs in its boot scope rather than inheriting a request's expiring operation lifetime. Sequential borrowed runtime and delivery requests continue only in their same still-active parent; foreign, expired and independent parents remain refused. These mechanisms were reproduced with original-source RED and repaired GREEN controls.

    The coherent delivery retains the earlier checkpoint, realtime and REST transfer reductions. Observed progress commits before ACK; completed output, versions and due state remain atomic. No clinical timer, authorization, restore or old-process effect fence was replaced with a counter or a longer deadline.
