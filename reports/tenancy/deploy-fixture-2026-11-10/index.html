    Core: what stayed, what went (f3dc10c8)¶

    Path kept as deploy/multitenant. The deploy repo's tools/compose-overlay.sh places its tree at that path over a core checkout, and every kept module requires core by ../../lib/...; a move under tests/fixtures/ would change both and break the overlay's byte comparison for no gain. The five kept JavaScript files are byte-identical to 16a1fc2d.

    restore.js required by demo.js; loaded by admin-lifecycle-fit

    ui.js loaded by compose-ui

    nightscout-deploy: what was ported (13f8bfb0, 085d88ad)¶

    Based on docs/operator-readme-20261110 (9387428), not main: the ownership-operations overlay (34417f8) the brief asks to keep is only on that branch.

    1. 13f8bfb0: bindings/compose/multitenant replaced by core's 16a1fc2d:deploy/multitenant, byte for byte (tree 6529d40282ea4589ea75c71712839d4db6e9940b; 4 files differed from the ed2ced00 import: bootstrap.js, config.js, health.js, restore.js). Pin moved to 16a1fc2d in README.md and bindings/compose/SOURCE.md.
    2. 085d88ad: ownership grants overlay-shaped. The base config.js drops ownership_operator from layout() and its block from grants(); the delta is recorded as bindings/compose/SOURCE.delta.patch. ownership-admin/ownership-grants.js gains core's tenant-phase read (phaseGrants, four statements), which core added after e6f6e248 and the first overlay lacked. tools/compose-identity-check.sh now applies the delta to core's tree at the recorded commit and compares; SOURCE.md records both hashes (here: 93a4a972f8cb6c6a974c943388d78783148af9f5).

    The decision between this and a plain byte-identical re-import (the earlier overlay README's plan: retire ownership-grants.js once the import carries the role) sits on the second commit alone; dropping 085d88ad gives the byte-identical form.
