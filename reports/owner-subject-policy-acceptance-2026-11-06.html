    Controls and revocation boundary¶

    The immutable database capture records two 22-case positive archives on 3106, four independent one-guard source mutations, and a fresh restored 22-case archive.     The suite exercises real PostgreSQL locking in both orderings for configuration, policy, actor and role changes. A narrowing committed before proposal locks is observed before mutation. It also checks exact SQL column privileges, forced RLS, tenant isolation, copied proposals, unknown roles/defaults, provider and current-database outages, recovery, final-audit rollback and legacy/default compatibility.     The final full campaign recorded 18 created containers and 26 volume references; the final browser campaign recorded 3 containers and 5 volume references. No named volume was authored by these runs, and no unrelated resource was selected for cleanup.     Scope retained for integration¶

    • The option is dormant unless explicitly supplied to the existing store. The separately authenticated JSON factory gains no automatic listener or identity issuer. Data/root credentials are not management authority.
    • Current selected grants are restricted to 37 reviewed REST operations. Shiro still implies descendants outside this universe; future endpoint permissions require review. Wildcard roles and the readable default refuse in this subset.
    • Complete catalogue/default semantics, role CRUD, existing-grant clamping, selected removal/ownership lifecycle, human identity/session/renewal and durable stream revocation remain separate work. Selected removal explicitly refuses.
    • Reverse-order operator transactions may deadlock and roll back. Arbitrary lock ordering, a total transaction deadline and client-cancellation guarantees are not established by these checks.
    • The earlier authorization-pipeline benchmark remains a separately scoped measurement. No production capacity, milestone promotion, RC admission or production enablement follows from this source acceptance.

    The coordinator owns cumulative integration into PR #8898, canonical owner-authority links and release/queue/dashboard/map records. Cumulative composition requires its own measured regression; this record does not relabel the independent 17f9 result onto a combined application head. Publication remains a maintainer action.
