    Milestone promotion review: M0–M3 against the recorded evidence (2026-11-06)¶

    Record

    Record as of 2026-11-06. A point-in-time document; it is not kept up to date.

    Retained record, archived 2026-11-10 from alignment c53105eeeb04225910e3c6871629fed5c7b78853. The captured text below preserves its read-derived M0–M3 gap review against R24–R55. Accepted M0–M5 criteria are recorded in the delivery dashboard and the integration record. Do not act on its “next” or open questions.

    What this is: a read-derived review, batch B4 of the integration batching plan. It maps every criterion in the "Acceptance before promotion" column of the roadmap's section 4 table for M0, M1, M2 and M3 to the evidence in the RC 16.0.0 integration record entries R24–R55 and the dashboard's accepted-slices table. Anchored on alignment main c57589e9b553af0dafdcce49d96b3ad4b448a30c, read at the start of the review. This document promotes nothing. No campaign was run for it; every count and commit below is quoted from the record text, and a status of measured means a record reports the measurement, not that this review repeated it. Where a roadmap cell packs several properties into one sentence they are split into atomic rows. Draft requiring maintainer review.

    How to read the tables¶

    status meaning here
    measured a record in R24–R55, a dashboard row, or a linked roadmap evidence section reports the property exercised with a stated count or a named red control
    partial measured for a bounded case or on a lane head, with a named part of the criterion not covered
    unmeasured no record reports it, or the record itself says UNMEASURED or "unimplemented"

    Counts across campaigns overlap and are never summed; the records say so in each entry. Commit shorthands are the record's own. The roadmap's two cells that use the older word for the tenancy target are rendered here with the contract's vocabulary ("multitenant").
