    Command journeys: plans, confirmations and moment commands for one pump with many orchestrators¶

    Record

    Record as of 2026-10-18. A point-in-time document; it is not kept up to date.

    What this is: a journey map (contributor-facing). Read after the command queue and planning note, whose use cases and §5 decisions it walks step by step; the needs it finds are specified in commands (CM1–CM25) and plans (PN1–PN18). Snapshot — describes 2026-10-18. Status: proposed.

    Each journey follows the format of the 15.0.9 journey map: who is involved, then numbered steps. Each step names its level (policy, confirmation, moment or algorithm, as in the note's §3), then Do (what happens), Working (what correct looks like), Watch for (the failure a tester or reviewer looks for) and Contributor (the rules that apply, the existing conformance case under specs/sync/conformance/, the unsafe control actions from the control-loop analysis, and what is not specified, with the open item or decision it needs). Claims about a named controller are read-derived, as in the note.

    The journeys follow the maintainer's decisions of 2026-10-18 (note §5): a controller enacts each commandId at most once; plans run on the controller and the hub keeps a read-only mirror; a plan or proposal may require named reviews before approval; the person may withdraw or cancel any item on their own therapy; followers read the queue; an adjustment is a new proposal, and an approval may accept a plan in parts, naming the elements it accepts. Two dose checks apply: the hub refuses a proposal's approval when a dose or carb record reached it after the proposal was made (CM22), and the controller refuses at enactment by its own rule (CM23); Part 3 shows which serves each journey.

    Words used here¶

    word meaning
    queue item a proposal, a plan or a command on one tenant's channel, with its state
    plan a recurring intent with its trigger, conditions, bounds and end date, approved once and held and run by the controller (note §3)
    element one separately acceptable part of a plan, such as an eating-soon target or a meal entry
    occurrence one run of a plan, such as Tuesday's lunch
    confirmation a small command against one occurrence, sent hours ahead and bounded by the plan
    mirror the hub's read-only copy of a plan and its occurrences, written from the controller's reports
    since check comparing what changed (doses, carbs, intents, the definition) since an item was made
    hub refuses the hub refuses a proposal's approval when a dose or carb record reached it after the proposal was made (CM22); it does not apply to a plan (PN7)
    controller decides the controller refuses a dose or carbs at enactment by its own rule (CM23), at each plan occurrence too (PN10); the outstanding list shows doses and carbs since (CM24)

    The people¶

    role does who, in these journeys
    proposer drafts an item (CM11, CM12) an agent with calendar, location or activity access; a clinician; a caregiver; the person
    evaluator or reviewer attaches evidence (CM16); a required reviewer must do so before a plan's approval (PN6) a risk-check agent, a second agent, a caregiver, a clinician
    approver turns an item into a command or an active plan (CM13, CM14) the person, or a delegate under a grant (authority §7)
    the person may approve, adjust (by a new proposal), withdraw or cancel any item on their own therapy (CM14, CM25), skip an occurrence or end a plan (PN14, PN16) the person with diabetes, or a parent for a young child
    enactor the singleton controller holding the channel's command-sink; validates, enacts once, reports the AID app on the person's phone (authority §6)
    follower reads the queue, the mirror and outcomes (CM24, PN11); never acts a partner, a school nurse with read access, a watch app

    Part 1: planning journeys¶

    PL1. A weekday lunch, planned from the calendar and location¶

    proposer reviewer approver the person enactor
    a planning agent a risk-check agent; a caregiver the person approves in parts; may skip any day the controller 
    sequenceDiagram
  accTitle: PL1, a weekday lunch planned from the calendar and location
  accDescr: A planning agent drafts a plan with an eating-soon element and a meal element. A risk-check agent and a caregiver review it. The person accepts the eating-soon element and declines the meal, preferring to bolus at the table. The controller holds the plan and the hub mirrors it. Each morning the agent confirms the day from the calendar and location; on a day the meeting runs over the person skips it; otherwise the controller starts eating soon after its own checks and reports it.
  participant G as Planning agent
  participant R as Reviewers
  participant P as Person
  participant H as Hub (mirror)
  participant C as Controller
  G->>H: plan: eating soon + meal, weekdays, end date (PL1.1)
  R->>H: risk check, caregiver review (PL1.2)
  P->>H: accept eating soon, decline meal (PL1.3)
  H->>C: the accepted plan (PL1.4)
  C->>H: holds it, mirror active
  G->>H: morning: lunch on the calendar, usual place (PL1.5)
  H->>C: confirmation for today
  alt meeting runs over
    P->>H: skip today (PL1.6)
    H->>C: skip, confirmation cancelled
  else lunch happens
    Note over C: own checks (PL1.7)
    C->>H: eating soon ran
  end

    PL1.1 · policy · The agent drafts the plan. - Do: the agent sees the usual lunch at the usual time and place over past weeks and proposes a plan with two elements: an eating-soon target before lunch, and the usual lunch carbs with their meal bolus; weekdays only, each occurrence needing a confirmation that morning, with an end date. - Working: the queue shows one open plan proposal: its elements, trigger, bounds, end date, the proposing agent and its expiry, and an evaluation saying in words why (no calendar entries). - Watch for: calendar titles or places in the proposal or evaluation; a plan with no end date. - Contributor: CM11, CM12 (propose-with-permission-and-role, propose-without-role), CM16, authority DR7; UCA-PROP-001, UCA-PROP-004. The plan object: plans PN1–PN4 (plan-proposed-and-approved, plan-ends-missing); a plan proposal stays open up to plans.maxProposalSeconds, not CM3's commands.maxExpirySeconds (PN1, plan-proposal-open-past-command-expiry). Not specified: the trigger grammar (plans §9 item 3) and the grammar of an element's content (commands §9 item 3).

    PL1.2 · policy · Required reviews. - Do: the plan names two required reviews: a risk-check agent and a caregiver. Each attaches an evaluation. - Working: an approval before both are attached is refused 409 review-required; each review shows who made it, and a review by the proposer does not count. - Watch for: an approval accepted with a required review missing; a review that changes the proposal's state. - Contributor: CM16 (evaluation-does-not-change-proposal, evaluate-without-read); plans PN6 (approve-review-missing, approve-review-by-proposer-not-counted): the proposer names them in requiredReviews (PN1), and a plan accepting an R3 or higher element needs at least one; UCA-PROP-011. Not specified: whether a second agent run by the same person or operator counts (plans §9 item 7).

    PL1.3 · policy · The person accepts part of it. - Do: the person accepts the eating-soon element and declines the meal element, preferring to bolus at the table. - Working: the approval names the element accepted and the hash it binds to; the declined element is recorded as declined; one plan, with one element, reaches the controller. - Watch for: the declined element reaching the controller; an approval refused because the person's intents changed between drafting and approval (an unrelated override). - Contributor: plans PN5, PN7, PN8 (approve-one-of-two-elements, approve-element-hash-mismatch): the approval names each accepted element by id and hash, the rest are recorded as declined, and the command carries the accepted elements under one hash; PN6 and CM14 (approve-own-agent-plan-refused); a plan's basedOn is per channel, so a change on a channel no accepted element acts on does not stale it (PN7, approve-plan-unrelated-channel-changed, approve-plan-state-changed); UCA-PROP-003, UCA-PROP-006. Not specified: approver signatures over the accepted elements (plans §9 item 5).

    PL1.4 · policy · The controller takes the plan; the hub mirrors it. - Do: the approval becomes one command; the controller validates the plan against its own limits, holds it and reports succeeded. - Working: the command reads succeeded; the mirror moves from approved to active on that report and shows the accepted and declined elements; a redelivered copy of the command is not stored twice. - Watch for: the mirror showing the proposal's content rather than what the controller holds; the plan stored again after two fetches before its report. - Contributor: CM1, CM7–CM10, CM18, CM21 (enact-identical-payload-succeeds, enact-hash-mismatch-refused, enact-refetched-once); plans PN8, PN9, PN11 (plan-held-active, plan-refused-by-controller, plan-command-expires-at-ends): the hub keeps the mirror and moves the controller's states only on the controller's reports; UCA-CMD-006, UCA-PROP-008. Not specified: the plan channel a product declares (plans §9 item 6).

    PL1.5 · confirmation · The morning confirmation. - Do: at a set hour the agent reads today's calendar and location and confirms today's occurrence, with an evaluation of one line ("lunch on the calendar, at the usual place"). - Working: a confirmation command for this occurrence, expiring at the occurrence's time, appears in the queue; the mirror shows the occurrence as confirmed. - Watch for: a confirmation carrying anything outside the plan's bounds; a confirmation for a plan that was ended; an agent able to send commands outside plans. - Contributor: CM3, CM12; plans PN12 (confirm-by-proposer, confirm-by-other-subject, confirm-too-early, confirm-after-role-lost): the approved plan is its proposer's grant to confirm from its proposing instance without a further approval, and a confirmation never releases a dose by itself; UCA-AUTH-002, UCA-AUTH-003.

    PL1.6 · moment · The lunch is cancelled. - Do: the meeting runs over; the person skips today's occurrence from the phone or the hub. - Working: the occurrence reads skip-requested; its confirmation is cancelled if not handed over, and a cancel request if it was; the controller reports skipped, or ran if it had already run; tomorrow's occurrence is untouched. - Watch for: a skip that ends the whole plan; a skip read as done before the controller reports. - Contributor: CM2, CM3; plans PN14 (skip-by-person, skip-cancels-pending-confirmation, skip-then-controller-reports-skipped), cancelling as CM25 does; UCA-CMD-015, FB-CMD-001.

    PL1.7 · algorithm · The occurrence runs. - Do: at the planned time the controller starts the eating-soon target, after its own checks of what was dosed and eaten since the confirmation. - Working: the hub shows the target active, with initiator {value: other, controllerTerm} naming the plan, and the mirror links the occurrence to its intent. - Watch for: the target shown as applied when only requested; the target started twice. - Contributor: S8, S10, A2, A3, A10, A12 (active-state/requested-and-applied); FB-STATE-004, UCA-CMD-010; PN10, PN17; the controller's ran report carries the identity of the intent that shows it (PN11, plan-report-occurrence-ran). Not specified: a field on the intent naming its plan and occurrence; no open item in commands §9 or plans §9 carries it.

    PL2. Exercise from the calendar¶

    proposer reviewer approver the person enactor
    a planning agent none required the person may cancel any session the controller

    PL2.1 · policy · Plan proposed and approved whole. The agent proposes a temporary target before calendar entries marked as exercise, confirmed by location or activity; the person approves. Working: one active plan in the mirror; a target the controller announces ahead reads scheduled, not active (S4). Contributor: CM11–CM14, PN1–PN5, PN7 (plan-proposed-and-approved), S4. Precedent: AndroidAPS automation triggers on time, location and activity and can start a temporary target (note §3). Not specified: conditions on calendar, location or activity in the trigger (plans §9 item 3).

    PL2.2 · confirmation · Confirmed by activity. - Do: the agent sees the person arrive at the gym and confirms the occurrence. - Working: the confirmation is within the plan's bounds and expires at the occurrence's time; the evaluation says "at the usual place", not where. - Watch for: location data in the queue; a confirmation arriving after the session ended. - Contributor: CM3, CM16, DR7; PN12 (confirm-by-proposer): a confirmation at or after the occurrence's time is 409 occurrence-passed; UCA-CMD-003.

    PL2.3 · confirmation · The session does not happen. The entry stays on the calendar; the person stays home; no confirmation is sent. Working: the occurrence ends unconfirmed and nothing is enacted, and the controller reports the occurrence missed. Watch for: an occurrence run without a required confirmation. Contributor: HAZ-1; PN10, PN11. Not specified: how a plan states that an occurrence runs only when confirmed, part of the trigger grammar (plans §9 item 3).

    PL2.4 · algorithm · Two intents at once. - Do: the target starts while an override the person set is active. - Working: the hub reports both, with their initiators; the controller's combined result, if it publishes one, is the only composed value shown. - Watch for: the hub computing a combined target, or ending one intent with the other. - Contributor: S1, S3, A1, A9 (active-state/exclusive-overlap-reported); FB-STATE-003.

    PL3. A caregiver's week of school overrides¶

    proposer reviewer approver the person enactor
    a parent a school nurse (follower, may evaluate) the second parent a teenager, may cancel the controller

    PL3.1 · policy · A week planned on Sunday. - Do: a parent proposes five occurrences of a preset override around the timetable's sports lessons, referring to the preset by its controller id. - Working: the proposal records the destination's definitionId; the nurse can read it. - Watch for: a preset referred to by a name the controller can rename. - Contributor: CM11, PN1, S9 (idField, referencedBy); UCA-PROP-008. The nurse reads the proposal with api:proposals:read (CM11), and the outstanding list and the mirror with any of CM24's read permissions (PN11, plan-read-with-proposals-read, plan-read-without-permission).

    PL3.2 · policy · The other parent approves. - Do: the second parent approves. The proposing parent may also approve a plan they made from their own app (CM14); a plan with an R3 or higher element still needs a review from another subject first (PN6). - Working: one approval, one command, the plan held by the controller and mirrored. - Watch for: two approvals at once creating two plans. - Contributor: CM13, CM14 (approve-own-proposal-by-person, approve-approved-proposal; self-approval-refused applies to an agent's proposal), PN5, PN6 (approve-r4-without-any-review); UCA-PROP-009, UCA-PROP-013. Not specified: that the change from open to approved is atomic under concurrent approvals (commands §9 item 7).

    PL3.3 · policy · Wednesday changes. A school trip moves Wednesday's lesson; the parent proposes an adjusted plan, a new proposal. Working: once approved it replaces the held plan, and the mirror shows which plan is current. Watch for: both plans running on Wednesday. Contributor: decision 4; S3 supersedes, A7; PN16 ends the held plan. Not specified: an adjusted plan replacing one the controller holds, and several plans active on one channel (plans §9 item 10).

    PL3.4 · algorithm · The controller was updated midweek. - Do: the app updates and renews under a new definition in which the preset changed. - Working: the controller refuses Thursday's occurrence with a reason the hub records, and the parents see it. - Watch for: the occurrence enacted under the changed preset with no notice. - Contributor: CM8, S12, registration §8; UCA-PROP-008. A plan approved after a renewal under a new definition is refused 409 definition-changed (PN7, approve-plan-definition-changed); a renewal after the approval does not affect it (approve-plan-definition-unchanged), so the controller's check at the occurrence catches this (PN10) and it reports the occurrence refused with its reason (PN11). For a moment proposal the definition at approval is open (commands §9 item 7).

    PL4. A sensitivity plan for illness or a cycle phase, with an end date¶

    proposer reviewer approver the person enactor
    a planning agent a named human reviewer (a clinician or caregiver) the person may end it early the controller

    PL4.1 · policy · Proposed with an end date and a disclosure. - Do: the agent proposes a sensitivity change for the coming days, ending on a date, labelled for display with the effect only. - Working: the queue shows the effect and the end date; the reason is visible only as the person's disclosure allows. - Watch for: the motivation (illness, cycle phase) shown to followers. - Contributor: CM11; state and intent §5 (disclosure, proposed, not adopted). Not specified: disclosure on queue items and the mirror, which waits on state and intent §8 (effect and motivation).

    PL4.2 · policy · A required human review, then approval. The reviewer attaches a review and the person approves; Working and Contributor as PL1.2–PL1.4.

    PL4.3 · algorithm · Runs and ends. The controller applies the change each day and reports it. Working: active each day; the controller reports the plan ended at endsAt and no occurrence runs after it. Contributor: S5, A4, A12, PN10, PN15 (plan-ended-by-controller).

    PL4.4 · moment · The person ends it early. - Do: feeling better, the person ends the plan. - Working: the mirror reads ending and the plan's queued occurrence commands are cancelled; the controller ends the active change with cancelled, stops future occurrences and reports the plan ended. - Watch for: future occurrences still listed; the change still active on the pump. - Contributor: S5, A5; plans PN16 (end-plan-by-person, end-cancels-pending-confirmation, end-unconfirmed-plan): the person, the approver or the proposer may end a plan, by an end command to the controller; CM14, CM25 for other items. Not specified: who counts as the person (commands §9 item 7).

    PL5. A clinician's settings proposal for the next visit¶

    proposer reviewer approver the person enactor
    a clinician a risk-check agent; the person's caregiver the person, at the visit approves in parts the controller

    PL5.1 · policy · Proposed weeks ahead. - Do: the clinician proposes changes to several settings, to be discussed at a visit weeks away. - Working: the proposal is readable to the person and the reviewers until the visit. - Watch for: the proposal expiring before the visit, or a hub limit raised so far that every command could live that long. - Contributor: CM11, CM3 (proposal-expiry-too-long); plans PN1: a plan proposal stays open up to plans.maxProposalSeconds, separate from the command limit (plan-proposal-open-past-command-expiry, plan-proposal-expiry-too-long); UCA-AUTH-006. Not specified: how a capability document marks a clinician's propose role (commands §9 item 1).

    PL5.2 · policy · Reviews before the visit. A risk-check agent compares the proposal with recent history; a caregiver adds notes. Working: evaluations attached, none changing the proposal. Contributor: CM16; as PL1.2.

    PL5.3 · policy · Accepted in part at the visit. - Do: the person accepts one setting change and declines the others. - Working: the approval names the accepted element; the declined ones stay on record. - Watch for: a state-changed refusal because an override started that morning. - Contributor: PN5, PN7: a plan's basedOn is per channel, so the override stales it only when an accepted element acts on the override's channel (approve-plan-other-channel-override); as PL1.3.

    PL5.4 · algorithm · The controller applies it under its own limits. Working: its next settings upload shows the change; the command reads succeeded. Watch for: the hub's stored settings changed before the controller reports. Contributor: CM7, S10, D18; a settings element is R5 in the draft classes, so it cannot be standing (PN4) and its occurrence needs the person's own approval (PN13, approve-r5-occurrence-by-person). Not specified: a settings change is not a span intent, and no controller declares accepting one as a command.

    Part 2: moment journeys¶

    C1. A correction bolus for a child at school¶

    proposer reviewer approver the person enactor
    — — a parent (sends directly) the child the controller

    C1.1 · moment · The parent looks before sending. - Do: the parent reads glucose, insulin on board and the outstanding list. - Working: the list shows any open proposal and any command on the channel not yet final, with its sender, and the dose and carb records that reached the hub since the earliest of them. - Watch for: insulin on board from before a dose the controller already gave. - Contributor: CM24 (outstanding-lists-open-items, outstanding-excludes-final); UCA-SYNC-SERVE-004, UCA-CMD-001.

    C1.2 · moment · Sends a correction bolus with a requestId and a short expiry. Working: one command, pending then fetched; a retried send returns the first. Contributor: CM1–CM5 (request-id-same-payload-returns-first, expiry-required).

    C1.3 · algorithm · Enacted once, after the controller's checks. - Do: the controller receives the command by fetch, after a fetch-now signal or on its cycle, and again by its next fetch before it reports; it checks hash, expiry and its own limits and recent doses, and enacts it once. Today Trio refuses a remote bolus when boluses since the command's timestamp reach a fifth of the request; Loop's remote bolus checks the amount and the one-time password and no doses since. - Working: one bolus; the second delivery is recognised by commandId. - Contributor: CM10, CM18, CM21, CM26 (enact-refetched-once, enact-after-restart-once), CM23 (enact-dose-refused-doses-since); UCA-CMD-006, UCA-CMD-010.

    C1.4 · moment · The outcome. The controller reports succeeded; later the pump fails the delivery. Working: the parent sees the failure. Watch for: succeeded standing after it. Contributor: CM9 (report-after-final-refused); UCA-CMD-013. Not specified: a further state after succeeded (commands §9 item 4).

    C2.2 · moment · The meal is refused at the table. - Do: the caregiver cancels before the phone has fetched it. - Working: the command reads cancelled, final, never handed over. - Watch for: a cancel reported as done after the command was fetched; there it is only a request. - Contributor: CM2, CM3, CM25 (cancel-pending-is-final, cancel-after-fetch-is-a-request); UCA-CMD-015.

    C2.3 · algorithm · Already handed over. - Do: the controller had stored the carbs and refused the bolus, as Trio's meal command can. - Working: the outcome shows carbs entered and bolus refused, and the caregiver removes the carbs as a separate action. - Contributor: CM9, CM25; UCA-CMD-011. Not specified: an outcome per part (commands §9 item 4).

    C3. Two caregivers act for one meal¶

    proposer reviewer approver the person enactor
    — — a parent; a school nurse the child the controller

    C3.1 · moment · Both send. A parent and a nurse each send a meal bolus minutes apart. Working: the second sender sees the first command in the outstanding list, with who sent it. Watch for: two commands accepted with neither sender told.     P1. The person doses while a proposal is outstanding¶

    proposer reviewer approver the person enactor
    an agent — a caregiver doses on the phone the controller

    P1.1 · moment · Proposal made. An agent proposes a correction bolus with basedOn. Contributor: CM11.

    P1.2 · moment · The person boluses locally. The dose reaches the hub one upload later. Contributor: A12 covers intents, not doses; CM22 covers doses at approval; FB-STATE-006.

    P1.3 · moment · The caregiver approves. - Working: when the dose reached the hub after the proposal was made, the approval is refused 409 doses-since and the proposal stays open; a dose not yet uploaded is the controller's to catch at enactment. - Watch for: an approval accepted after the dose reached the hub (UCA-PROP-012). - Contributor: CM15 (approve-state-unchanged), CM22 (approve-doses-since, approve-doses-before-proposal), CM23 (enact-dose-refused-doses-since). CM22 catches a dose already uploaded; CM23 catches it at enactment on a controller with a since check.

    P2. The person sees and withdraws or cancels¶

    proposer reviewer approver the person enactor
    an agent; a caregiver — — withdraws and cancels the controller

    P2.1 · any · Opens the queue. The person sees proposals, plans and commands on their channels. Contributor: CM6, CM11, CM24 (outstanding-lists-open-items), PN11 and PN18 for plans.

    P2.2 · any · Withdraws an agent's proposal. Working: the proposal reads withdrawn. Contributor: CM14 lets the proposer and the person withdraw (withdraw-by-person, withdraw-by-other-subject). Not specified: who counts as the person, and whether there may be more than one (commands §9 item 7).

    A1. Carbs proposed from a photo of the meal¶

    proposer reviewer approver the person enactor
    an agent a risk-check agent the person adjusts the controller

    A1.1 · moment · Proposed. The agent proposes carbs with an evaluation of its estimate. Watch for: units or product misread on the approval screen. Contributor: CM11, CM16; UCA-PROP-004. Not specified: payload grammar (commands §9 item 3).

    A1.2 · moment · The person adjusts. The adjusted amount is a new proposal (decision 4), which the person may approve because they made it from their own app, or send as a command; either way they withdraw the agent's. Contributor: CM14 (approve-own-proposal-by-person, withdraw-by-person).

    A1.3 · algorithm · Enacted. The controller checks carbs since; Trio refuses when newer carbs exist and misses a back-dated meal. Contributor: CM10, CM23; UCA-CMD-010.

    Further cases, briefly¶

    • C4, an override or temporary target now: CM1–CM5; the since check is intents (A12, CM15 for a proposal). C5, closed loop or automatic bolus off: a running-mode channel; LoopCaregiver sends these to /api/v2/remotecommands, which no hub implements (commands §3).
    • A3, too many proposals: UCA-PROP-002; not specified: a limit per proposing instance and channel (commands §9 item 7).
    • U1, the controller's own automation: outside the hub; Trio's Shortcuts use a six-minute window. The mirror would show such plans only if the controller reports them.
    • U2, a downloaded careportal entry: no expiry, destination or outcome (UCA-SYNC-DL-001, state and intent §8).
    • F1, a follower: reads proposals, plans, the mirror and outcomes; any write is 403. Any of api:commands:read, api:commands:create or api:proposals:read reads the outstanding list and the mirror (CM24, PN11; outstanding-lists-open-items, plan-read-with-proposals-read).

    The since check and the dose checks¶

    journey since check which check serves it
    PL1 intents per channel at approval; doses and carbs at each occurrence controller decides (CM23, PN10): the occurrence runs hours after any hub check; hub refuses does not apply to a plan (PN7)
    PL2 intents; confirmation freshness controller decides (PN10); a confirmation expires at the occurrence's time (PN12)
    PL3 definition unchanged definition-changed at a plan's approval (PN7); after it, the controller (PN10, PN11)
    PL4 intents; definition controller decides (PN10); PN7 at approval
    PL5 definition; long expiry PN7 at approval; PN1 bounds the proposal's open time; doses are not what changes over weeks
    C1 doses since; outstanding controller decides (CM23); a command is not a proposal, and CM5 keeps the hub from judging it; CM24 shows doses since
    C2 carbs and doses since; cancel controller decides (CM23), with cancel (CM25)
    C3 doses since; outstanding, who sent controller decides (CM23), with the list showing the other sender (CM24)
    P1 doses and carbs since the proposal hub refuses (CM22) catches an uploaded dose; controller decides (CM23) catches it only on a controller with a since check
    A1 carbs since hub refuses (CM22) and controller decides (CM23); Trio's check misses back-dated meals
    A2 doses and carbs since; preview hub refuses (CM22) before approval; controller decides (CM23) at enactment, best with a preview (commands §9 item 2)
    C4, C5 intents CM15 for a proposal
    U1, U2, F1 none at the hub neither

    Hub refuses serves only P1, A1 and A2, and only when the local dose reached the hub before the approval. Controller decides serves every journey in which a controller checks doses since, which Loop's remote bolus path does not today.

    What the journeys cannot show without a controller implementation¶

    • whether a controller holds, runs and reports a plan, and the end reason of an unconfirmed or refused occurrence;
    • whether enact once survives a restart on each controller;
    • what a cancel after hand-over achieves;
    • the since checks a controller makes at enactment, and how often they refuse;
    • a preview before approval, outside AndroidAPS's client control;
    • whether the mirror stays in step with what the controller holds.
