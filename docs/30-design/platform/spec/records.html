    L1 Records: the typed observations a hub stores, and how today's documents decompose into them¶

    Living document, last changed 2026-10-18 at 13259e01d.

    Contributor-facing. Living specification, current at main 92a7d31b (2026-10-18). Layer L1 of the platform index; terms are in the glossary; record ids are in the identity specification. Status: evidenced, not implemented: the catalogue is generated from a shipped typed model and the corpus. Decision: D11, for devicestatus; the rest of this page is proposed (§2). Queue: SPEC-RECORDS.

    Specification 2 of 8 in the reading order; after identity, before registration, which declares what these records mean. Expect rules R1–R9, the generated catalogue of primitives and their evidence grades, decomposition, settings snapshots, and named test checks.

    A controller sends what it has, in the shape it has it: a treatment, a device status, a profile. The hub keeps that document and also decomposes it into typed observation primitives, so queries stay bounded and a dose can be replayed against the inputs that produced it. This page says which primitives exist, how much evidence stands behind each field, how a composite document is decomposed, and what a settings snapshot must carry for a replay to be faithful.

    1. Files¶

    file what it is
    specs/sync/primitives.yaml the observation catalogue, every field graded; generated by make schema-primitives (tools/nsschema/primitives.py)
    specs/sync/controller-settings.schema.json the effective-dated settings snapshot (§5)
    specs/nsschema/dosing-input-sources.yaml every dosing input each algorithm family declares, with where it is recorded
    specs/sync/sensitivity.yaml the two-axis sensitivity labels and the disclosure profiles (§6)
    specs/nsschema/*.model.json today's collections as stored, from the census
    specs/conformance/observability-profile.yaml the observability obligations a registration formalises

    2. Rules¶

    # rule
    R1 Composite on the wire, primitive in storage. The hub decomposes a written document into observation primitives and keeps the composite beside them, so "what did the device send" stays answerable and a legacy reader can be answered from the composite. A treatment carrying insulin and carbs becomes a Bolus and a CarbIntake sharing a correlation id; a device status becomes an ApsSnapshot, a PumpSnapshot and an UploaderSnapshot; a profile becomes TherapySettings and four schedules.
    R2 Decomposition is declared, not hard-coded. What a document decomposes into is declared by its registration: documents[].decomposesTo with a structural discriminator ({path: loop, test: is-object}), never the free-text device string (registration §3). The decomposition is data, so it does not become another list that drifts beside the API description, the indexes and the census model.
    R3 Decomposition sits above the storage seam. It runs for single-tenant and multi-tenant storage alike, as type coercion does. Only devicestatus's indexed fields become columns; its other nodes stay in the stored document, and the decomposed series are separate relations with their own schemas.
    R4 Every field carries an evidence grade. measured: a wire path in the corpus populates it, or the shipped decomposer maps it from a path the corpus carries. declared: a typed model declares it and the corpus shows no source. proposed: this programme asks for it and no shipped model has it. A grade is evidence, not endorsement; a replay or a consumer treats a declared field as unverified.
    R5 Settings are a snapshot, effective-dated and versioned with the build. A controller publishes a ControllerSettings snapshot on change, not per cycle, with effectiveFrom (and effectiveUntil when known) and the controller's product and version. A setting that changed mid-history invalidates a replay that assumed one value, and algorithm behaviour changes between releases.
    R6 Absent is not a default. Every setting is optional. A setting a controller does not publish is recorded as not published and never filled with a default value, because "not published" is information a replay needs.
    R7 Configuration and per-cycle state are separate. Settings hold configuration. Values a controller computes each cycle (the meal-detection slopes, flatBGsDetected, IOB components, the effective sensitivity and carb ratio) are not settings; their place is the cycle's record, and the scheduled forms belong to the schedule primitives. ApsSnapshot does not carry the meal-detection slopes or flatBGsDetected today (§3, fields no primitive carries).
    R8 Replay inputs are declared with their status. A registration lists every dosing input the controller could supply (replayInputs), with its status (recorded, partial, derivable, absent) and path; an input it has but does not publish has a null path. Completeness is then a published number per controller, not an estimate. A derived input is recomputed from stored history and is not the value the controller used; recorded and derivable are never reported as one figure.
    R9 Every field has a sensitivity label. A field is labelled on two axes, a level and a category (§6). In the label assignment that generates the schemas, an unlabelled field is identifying: a field is argued down, never silently up. In a registration, a declared field with no label falls under sensitivity.unlabelledPolicy, withhold when absent (registration G2).

    Status. R2 and R3 are D11 for devicestatus, where D11 also says a known catalogue ships with Nightscout and a controller may publish or correct its own. Applying R1–R3 to every collection, and R4–R9, are proposed: the catalogue (0.1-draft), the settings schema and the sensitivity labels are drafts for the maintainer and the controller authors.

    3. The catalogue¶

    Sixteen primitives, each a typed class in Nocturne's V4 model. Generated from specs/sync/primitives.yaml.

    primitive group typed by fields measured declared what it is
    SensorGlucose observation Nocturne V4 SensorGlucose.cs 14 8 6 A CGM reading.
    BGCheck observation Nocturne V4 BGCheck.cs 5 4 1 A fingerstick or meter reading.
    Bolus delivery Nocturne V4 Bolus.cs 13 6 7 A discrete insulin dose, manual or algorithm-initiated.
    TempBasal delivery Nocturne V4 TempBasal.cs 14 6 8 A temporary basal rate for a bounded interval.
    CarbIntake delivery Nocturne V4 CarbIntake.cs 5 2 3 A carbohydrate entry.
    ApsSnapshot decision Nocturne V4 ApsSnapshot.cs 25 23 2 One controller cycle: the state it saw and what it decided. The record a replay is checked against.
    PumpSnapshot device Nocturne V4 PumpSnapshot.cs 13 11 2 Pump state at a point in time.
    UploaderSnapshot device Nocturne V4 UploaderSnapshot.cs 6 4 2 The uploading device's own state.
    DeviceEvent device Nocturne V4 DeviceEvent.cs 2 2 0 A site change, sensor start, cartridge change.
    TherapySettings settings Nocturne V4 TherapySettings.cs 18 8 10 The therapy configuration in force.
    BasalSchedule settings Nocturne V4 BasalSchedule.cs 2 1 1 Scheduled basal rates by time of day.
    CarbRatioSchedule settings Nocturne V4 CarbRatioSchedule.cs 2 1 1 Carb ratio by time of day.
    SensitivitySchedule settings Nocturne V4 SensitivitySchedule.cs 2 1 1 Insulin sensitivity by time of day.
    TargetRangeSchedule settings Nocturne V4 TargetRangeSchedule.cs 2 1 1 Glucose targets by time of day.
    Note narrative Nocturne V4 Note.cs 3 2 1 Free text attached to a time.
    StateSpan state Nocturne V4 StateSpan.cs 8 4 4 A time-ranged state: pump mode, override, data exclusion. The state machine, not a point event.

    Field matching against the corpus is by leaf name and by the decomposer's own assignments, read from its source (Iob = ds.OpenAps.Iob?.Iob). A measured grade that rests only on a generic leaf name is flagged low_confidence and still counted as measured; no field carries the flag in the catalogue as committed. The corpus was fetched through API v1, so fields only API v3 carries (identifier, srvCreated, srvModified) and the settings collection are not observable in it.

    Where the evidence is weak. TherapySettings and the four schedules, because settings mostly do not reach the corpus; the schedules' entry interiors are unexamined. Anything about AndroidAPS: the corpus has no AndroidAPS closed-loop site.

    Fields no primitive carries. Proposed, each a request to a controller's authors. "Input of" is the algorithm family whose dosing-input map names the input; Loop records the equivalents of three oref0 inputs (profile.maxBasal, profile.suspendThreshold, profile.dosingStrategy) under profile.loopSettings. The reason each is asked for is in specs/sync/primitives.yaml.

    4. Decomposition, measured¶

    collection what is known source
    treatments Nocturne's routing places 99.86% of treatments by eventType alone and 0.13% by the data present. The 22 unroutable documents all carry Suspend Pump, Nightscout's documented spelling; Nocturne's V4 decomposition keys on Pump Suspend, the words reversed, so fixing that constant routes every treatment in the corpus. This measures which branch a document takes, not the records each branch writes. Five leaf field names have no typed home, among them userEnteredAt on 10 of 11 sites, so a named extension location is still needed hub-sync §3.3; extensibility models §3
    devicestatus a typed model that reads only declared paths drops 56 of 166 observed paths, nine of them on 10 of 11 sites, because only the top level has an extension point; Loop's dose recommendation (bolusVolume on 26.1% of statuses, tempBasalAdjustment on 6.1%) and the majority pump shape (pump.bolusing, pump.suspended, 85.1%) are among them. Decomposition coverage is not measured devicestatus and profile fidelity §2
    profile the typed model drops 1 of 66 observed paths; Loop's loopSettings subtree was undeclared in this repository's 2025.1.0 specs when measured (2026-09-10). Decomposition coverage is not measured same; typed schema evidence §4.4
    entries the typed model drops 1 of 19 observed paths devicestatus and profile fidelity §2

    Every loss above is a nested field. A composite document nested more than one level deep needs an extension point at each level, or decomposition into separately typed records; R1–R3 choose the second. The activity collection already works that way in production: xDrip+ posts {type: "hr-bpm", bpm} and {type: "steps-total", steps}, discriminated by type.

    Not measured: whether a decomposed set recomposes into a document a legacy client accepts, which decides whether decomposition can sit behind the existing API.

    5. Settings snapshot¶

    ControllerSettings (controller-settings.schema.json) requires effectiveFrom, controller (product and version required; vendor and algorithmFamily optional) and settings; it allows effectiveUntil. It is written to the API v3 settings collection, so it needs no new endpoint. Its nineteen settings, with their status in the corpus:

    status today settings
    recorded dia, maxBasalRate, maxBolus, units
    partial dosingStrategy, maxBasal, recommendationType, suspendThreshold
    derivable maxDailyBasal
    absent automaticBolusApplicationFactor, carbAbsorptionModel, gradualTransitionsThreshold, includePositiveVelocityAndRC, maxActiveInsulinMultiplier, maxIob, microBolusAllowed, recommendationInsulinModel, useIntegralRetrospectiveCorrection, useMidAbsorptionISF

    Dosing safety limits reach Nightscout as settings from Loop, as profile.loopSettings.*; no oref0-family controller records maxIob there. An oref0 rig uploads its preferences, max_iob among them, with each device status (devicestatus.preferences; oref0 d219baf9 bin/ns-status.js:40-49,175-177): configuration repeated every cycle, not a snapshot published on change (R5). This is read from controller source together with the corpus: the corpus alone would credit Trio with limits it never writes, and every AndroidAPS statement on this page is read from source. A registration's settings.fields may name a setting or declare it typed, by typeRef to its ControllerSettings definition (registration G2).

    Completeness. Of the dosing inputs each family declares, the share Nightscout records (hub-sync §4.2):

    declared inputs recorded recorded or derivable
    Loop 20 20% 55%
    Trio and AndroidAPS (oref0 family) 40 50% 72%

    These are source-derived estimates of what is recorded, not measured replay accuracy.

    6. Sensitivity labels¶

    specs/sync/sensitivity.yaml labels a field on two axes:

    • level:
    • secret: credential or key material; never leaves the party that needs it.
    • identifying: directly identifies a person, install or device; stripped, or replaced by a stable, non-reversible pseudonym.
    • quasi-identifying: narrowing in combination; coarsened where the analysis allows.
    • descriptive: a measurement, setting or vocabulary; still health data.
    • category: credential, identity, device, health-measurement, health-derived, therapy-setting, temporal, location, free-text, vocabulary.

    A disclosure profile (full, clinical, replay, effect-only, research, schema-evidence) admits fields up to a level and within listed categories. The label assignments are derived from the redaction policy this repository applies and from the census. What may lawfully be disclosed under any profile needs qualified review.

    7. Open¶

    • Decomposition coverage of devicestatus and profile (§4), and round-tripping to legacy readers.
    • The ScheduleEntry interior of the four schedules.
    • Every AndroidAPS field, from data.
    • The proposed fields (§3), which only controller authors can publish (PROPOSAL-REG-CONTROLLERS).
    • ControllerSettings is open: its settings object allows any additional member, and it carries an untyped x-aid-extensions member. Controller-specific settings belong in typed registration extensions (registration G2).
    • make schema-primitives truncates a proposed field's reason at 160 characters and gives some partial inputs the reason "a dosing input no controller publishes".
