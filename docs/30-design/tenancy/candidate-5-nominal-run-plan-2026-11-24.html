    Planning the next nominal multitenant run¶

    Record

    Record as of 2026-11-24. A point-in-time document; it is not kept up to date.

    Proposed, 2026-11-24. A nominal run asks whether the expected mix of uploads, reads and viewers receives timely service on a deployment provisioned for that mix. Keep the offer, history, scoring windows and product objectives explicit. A fixed-resource optimization run asks how much one change improves that same workload. A resource increase earns a new deployment result. Credit a code improvement only in a matched comparison. Experiment policy · Candidate 5 results

    Choose the write workload¶

    Run the joined candidate on the expected live offer. Include recent delayed connector records and keep the original reading time when measuring their age. Count connector delivery from its schedule, durable output from API acceptance, and viewer receipt independently. Booting from an attested fixture with deep tenant histories exercises ordinary reads against that data. Reuse a verified historical base across the ladder. Append and verify enough genuine recent observations to cover the planned deadlines once, then restore that child for the points it covers. Record the fixture restore and evaluator restart phases separately. Nominal write-age decision · Clinical deadline

    Classify catch-up by the operation and the age of its records. The existing 12-hour outage burst is a recovery scenario even though its events are recent. For an older backlog or a bulk import/export and restore, measure completed documents, identity and order, recovery time and sustained live service on other tenants. The original clinical timestamps and historical client cursors matter in this scenario. Size serving storage for the retained and imported history. A two-day live writer can still query a tenant with years of data. Restore and client convergence

    Provision the service and the instrument¶

    Give the PostgreSQL primary a dedicated Kubernetes node. Set the database pool selector and its NoSchedule taint through the deployment binding’s postgresNode input. Place API, evaluators and realtime on the application nodes. The driver and controller can use application or separate instrument workers. Verify actual Pod placement on restore. Reproduced from the 3k placement receipts. All six API Pods, 32 evaluator Pods and eight realtime Pods ran on the two application nodes. The PostgreSQL Pod ran on a third node. Compare the successor on that topology. Primary placement decision

    Read-derived 3k trial allocation. Retain the measured 32 evaluator slices, eight realtime replicas, six API replicas, and eight data-pool seats per API for the first matched comparison. The deployment used two 16-vCPU/64-GiB application workers and a dedicated 16-vCPU/128-GiB database worker. PostgreSQL requested 12 CPUs and 64 GiB and used 32 GiB shared buffers. In the steady phase the cache hit rate was 99.98%, mean block read time was 0.430 ms and there were zero requested checkpoints. The busiest API pool averaged 4.30 of eight seats with zero checkout refusals. Raising its connection limit would also increase work in the frozen backend-identity query.

Preflight the exact native work deadline before allocating a case. Give collection and cleanup their own budgets. The 3k gate took 429.4 s, including a 300 s quiet-window convention. An earlier-start discovery run should continue observing backlog through settling and qualify its steady interval separately.     • Time budget. As an initial 3k planning allowance, reserve 20 minutes for setup from case entry, 40 minutes for ordinary traffic, 10 minutes for drain and collection, and 10 minutes for cleanup. The observed 3k foundation-to-first-request time was 12 min 42 s. At the observed prime rate, 10k needs roughly seven additional minutes just to open its viewers. Recalculate fixture eligibility and scheduling from actual phase deadlines. This example is separate from any live campaign grant.
    • Larger points. Recompute the app placement, viewer-source port and memory demand, driver concurrency and database CPU/statement/connection demand at each offer. Use the measured 3k per-role demand as a starting estimate. Allow sufficient workers for the requests to fit with ordinary system overhead. A larger fleet or independently scaled observer establishes a new nominal operating point. Keep a fixed-allocation replay for the optimization comparison.

    Score the outcomes independently¶

    Recommendation. Publish whether the workload completed, what users received, which product objectives it met and which evidence was unavailable. Record the strict HTTP result even when another measurement passes. At 3k, 13/89,202 steady responses were 503 (0.0146%, within the 0.1% aggregate objective), the zero steady storage-refusal target failed, and the busiest data pool exceeded its 50% planning target. Preserve those three facts even when the stage summary records only its first failure.

    Keep stored-write reconciliation, tenant authority, isolation, restore fencing and datastore health. Retain 30 s durable p95 at expected load, roughly 1 s stretch and the 120 s clinical ceiling from acceptance. Score browser freshness separately using 4/10/30 s bands, upper tails, unmatched and censored counts. Keep per-route HTTP p50/p95/p99, refusal phase, offered/issued/completed throughput, drops and end backlog. The 3k wrong-host socket probes received transport 403 before the application callback. Application-level isolation on those probes is unmeasured. Product failure criteria · Latency objectives · Performance signals

    For optimization, keep fixture content and clinical age, active roster, per-route offer, viewer distribution, topology, allocations and warm-up comparable. Build supported simplifications concurrently, run their affected checks and try the joined configuration at the target offer. Use a matched replay of one mechanism when a failed outcome or a specific performance claim needs attribution. Qualify the frozen candidate once. Qualification sequence

    For a nominal deployment, reserve a realistic envelope in advance and report actual usage and spare capacity. Reducing upload cadence, dropping viewer updates, serving stale status or delaying authority rechecks would each change what users receive and would require its own product decision. The measurements support implementation and harness changes first.
