    Multitenant integration batching plan¶

    Record

    Record as of 2026-11-06. A point-in-time document; it is not kept up to date.

    Contributor-facing living coordination plan, refreshed 2026-11-10. This page owns capability boundaries, integration order and verification depth. The queue owns claimed item state; the dashboard owns recorded milestone claims; release contents and the integration record own actual source, acceptance and publication. The retained batching journal preserves the original dispatch and all captured evidence.

    Completion-focused coordination¶

    Maintainer direction, 2026-11-06: organize lanes around complete capabilities and adopted milestone criteria. A helper, fixture repair or individual control belongs to its capability lane; an independently deliverable capability or material architectural unknown may have a separate owner.

    • Give each lane one observable deliverable, owner, exit criterion, focused positive/negative checks, unresolved decisions and next integration boundary. Confirm the head commit and that the task remains open before starting. Stop and report a mismatch rather than reaching for the nearest thing that works.
    • Run changed-code lint and affected/focused checks during implementation. Keep meaningful controls for isolation, authorization, clinical behavior and compatibility, followed by restoration. A setup failure is not a semantic red.
    • Resolve architectural unknowns with bounded early proofs whose question and stop condition are explicit. Incorporate their result into the capability; a proof does not become a separate comprehensive campaign.
    • Freeze the coherent joined candidate for one full nonbrowser, default-browser, enabled-corpus and applicable native/client campaign, then final prcheck. Preserve failed campaigns, diagnose narrowly, join planned repairs and repeat final acceptance when material inputs changed. A leaf commit, resumption or documentation update does not trigger a new comprehensive campaign.
    • Reuse evidence only with exact source/instrument attribution and an explicit affected-input analysis. Ancestry establishes incorporation, not execution. Source-identical evidence retains its original executed head and limits.
    • Maintain one evolving lane handoff and one programme acceptance update per coherent batch. Record substantive failures, decisions and ownership transfers promptly. Report capability/criterion closure, remaining blockers and the next integration owner with the evidence link; counts are supporting observations.

    1. Evidence and ownership¶

    Use the current acceptance bundle for the published restore/Compose candidate, and the M5/M6 decision index for adopted policy and open implementation/measurement work. A dated journal or local handoff does not assign new work. Confirm active ownership with the programme coordinator before claiming an existing lane; the release record links the evolving local handoffs.

    The source owner integrates original committed lane histories by fast-forward or ordinary merge. Check ancestry and patch equivalence first; resolve overlap explicitly. Do not cherry-pick/rebase patches and later merge their original history solely to make evidence commits reachable. Continue the existing draft #8898; the adopted integration direction does not authorize a cleanup rewrite or replacement PR.

    The original measured basis retains the older PR/CI snapshots. They are historical observations, not the current publication state.

    2. Acceptance ladder¶

    Stage Required evidence Record owner
    Locally accepted Focused capability checks and meaningful red/restored controls on the lane's actual head Capability owner, in the lane handoff
    Incorporated Original lane history is an ancestor of the joined candidate; overlap and affected checks resolved Source integration owner
    Integrated acceptance recorded Coherent frozen candidate passes its applicable regression/native/client campaign and final prcheck Acceptance coordinator, with exact source and instrument attribution
    Milestone promoted Every adopted milestone criterion has an explicit verdict; deferrals have an adopted basis Maintainer decision, reflected in dashboard/status sources

    Implementation, functional preview acceptance, publication, merge to dev, operator release and production/default enablement are separate claims. A promoted component or milestone supplies evidence for decisions within its tested scope; it does not establish capacity, high availability or a new deployment.

    3. Batch rules¶

    1. Keep default single/Mongo regression and explicit multitenant corpus coverage. Reconcile the enabled-suite inventory with source before each freeze; publish named pending/unsupported cases. A pending benchmark is not a functional pass.
    2. Changes to existing shipping paths, migrations, dependencies, tier registry or shared fixtures are assessed on the coherent joined candidate. Material changes invalidate the affected final acceptance; they do not require a full campaign for every intermediate commit.
    3. Run focused jobs only with positive ownership of schemas, ports, processes and containers. Local comprehensive campaigns share the coordinator's serialized runtime slot. Parallel native jobs need measured capacity and isolation; unknown resources remain untouched. Quiet performance work gets a separate controlled window.
    4. Use the complete opt-in corpus command for corpus acceptance; an archived gate or a filtered focused suite cannot establish whole-corpus acceptance. CI and local campaigns keep their actual source/environment attribution.
    5. Gates read; they never mutate another session's checkout. Update queue refs only after the applicable measured acceptance, retaining native evidence pins. Without a required database/runtime, report UNMEASURED, not green.
    6. Every capability brief includes source head, outcome, integration boundary, focused tests, meaningful controls and remaining decision boundaries, plus the standing confirm-head/open-task and stop-on-mismatch instructions above.
    7. Publication remains a separately authorized action. This plan supplies no authority to publish application source, enable defaults or admit real data.

    4. Retained dispatch¶

    The B1–B7 dispatch preserves original branch identities, commands, predictions, failures, controls and ownership. Those completed/older lanes are evidence inputs, not today's task list. The schedule below governs further work.

    2026-11-09: M6 split and capability items¶

    Stage C above is now four milestones and a production milestone, M6a–M6d and M7 (roadmap, decision). Each has one to four queue items with one gate each; the dashboard lists them with their claimed state. Native acceptance runs through a disposable harness; no lead reservation or grant is required.

    Criterion matrix for the next frozen candidate¶

    The coordinator records actual source, instrument, command/selector, artifact, control and verdict for each applicable roadmap acceptance family. Name the carried evidence, changed input and why reuse is valid for each retained criterion. Record explicit deferrals before milestone promotion. A count, a waiver inferred from a prototype, or an incorporated source change cannot replace that matrix.

    For production-quality deployment, keep four questions separate: whether the component behaves correctly under tested faults; whether its chosen deployment preserves those assumptions; whether its measured workload meets approved bounds; and whether it is admitted for release/operation. The deployment guide explains topologies and fault domains. Remaining measurement work continues under adopted policy; a deferred policy question retains its prototypes and benches.

    The production dependency map continues beyond C through actual deployment qualification and release/admission. It proposes sequencing and closing evidence, without assigning new lanes or changing the adopted milestone bars.

    6. Lane ownership and coordination¶

    The programme coordinator owns criterion closure, source freeze, queue/status integration. Each capability has one source owner and one next integration boundary. Native acceptance runs in a disposable harness that records the source head, requires a clean tree and removes its containers (decision); the earlier grant/reservation ceremony is retired.

    Local evolving handoffs remain under externals/work/HANDOFF-<lane>-<date>.md. The latest release update points to current combined ownership; the archived lane roster retains earlier responsibilities and traps. Never take an old “active” label as a present assignment. Preserve peer drafts and existing worktrees; inspect the shared index and commit only owned paths.

    7. Scope and retention¶

    This plan changes no adopted milestone bar, release placement, backend default, provider selection or operator admission. The full captured predecessor at alignment 8792fc7a3d62f59629ed94bac168dcad5d9aa3c7 is retained in the batching journal, including its unique measurements, failed predictions and historical assignments. Git retains intermediate revisions. Correct coordination here, decisions in their canonical home and new acceptance in release/integration records; do not append execution journals to the current schedule.
