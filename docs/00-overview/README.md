# Overview

*For the Nightscout Foundation board, the maintainers of the ecosystem projects, reviewers, and the
community. Living page, 2026-10-02.*

The overview is in three parts, so that a reader can build a picture of the ecosystem before meeting
any argument about what to do. Each part can be read without agreeing with the next.

## 1. Observations: what exists, and what has happened

Descriptive only: dates, counts, sources and methods, with no recommendation.

| page | what it covers |
|---|---|
| [History](observations/HISTORY.md) | a chronology from 2012 to now: projects, hosting changes, regulation and published evidence, with sources and the points where sources differ |
| [Governance today](observations/GOVERNANCE-TODAY.md) | how decisions are made now: who merges and releases in each project, written project governance, the foundation's public statements and processes, the shared provider listing, and what is not public |
| [Ecosystem evidence](observations/ECOSYSTEM-EVIDENCE.md) | what the ecosystem consists of, what depends on the shared server, release and contributor history across the main projects, where maintenance time goes, dates set by vendors and platforms, published clinical evidence |

## 2. Perspective: how other communities met the same conditions

Comparison and frames for reading the observations. Interpretation is labelled as interpretation,
and the volunteer-only view is stated beside the others.

| page | what it covers |
|---|---|
| [When a DIY ecosystem becomes infrastructure](perspective/INFRASTRUCTURE-TRANSITION.md) | the conditions under which other open-source communities changed how their work was organised, which of those conditions can be measured here, and what the frame does not show |
| [Precedents](perspective/PRECEDENTS.md) | one table of every organisation the proposals cite: how each is funded, who is paid, the commercial role, and size |

## 3. Recommendations: the author's proposals

Drafts for discussion, written by the maintainer of cgm-remote-monitor, who also founded a company
that hosts Nightscout commercially and could be a candidate for paid work under them. Each states
those interests and offers options, including no change. Nothing in them is decided.

| page | what it proposes |
|---|---|
| [Ecosystem programme](ECOSYSTEM-PROGRAMME.md) | the work as five layers, and which the foundation might steward |
| [Sponsored maintenance team](SPONSORED-TEAM-PROPOSAL.md) | a small paid team for review, release, compatibility and security work |
| [Working together](COLLABORATION-MODEL.md) | rules for volunteers, app projects, servers, hosts and researchers working together |
| [Quality system](QUALITY-SYSTEM.md) | a shared quality kit for every layer of the stack, and how its requirements would be agreed |
