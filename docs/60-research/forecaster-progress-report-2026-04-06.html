    §3. Clinical Scoring — All Experiments¶

    Approximate MARD Methodology¶

    True MARD requires per-sample computation: MARD = mean(|pred − true| / true × 100). Since stored results only contain aggregate MAE, we approximate:

    MARD_approx = MAE / μ_glucose × 100

    where μ_glucose = 155 mg/dL (population mean across 11 patients, σ=55).

    This is a lower bound by Jensen's inequality: E[|x|/y] ≥ E[|x|] / E[y]. True MARD is typically 10–30% higher than this approximation. For high-glucose patients (μ=180), actual MARD would be lower; for well-controlled patients (μ=130), it would be higher.

    §4. The ERA 2 → ERA 3 Gap¶

    What Changed Between Eras¶

    Factor ERA 2 (EXP-043–171) ERA 3 (EXP-352–398)
    Patients Single-patient 11-patient pooled
    Fine-tuning Per-patient Cross-patient (mostly)
    Horizons Single (1hr) Multi (h30/h60/h120)
    Architecture CNN GroupedEncoder transformer → various
    Best MAE 12.6 mg/dL @ 1hr 23.7 mg/dL @ 1hr
    MARD ~8.1% ~15.3%

    Root Cause Analysis¶

    1. Per-patient fine-tuning (ERA 2) vs cross-patient pooling (ERA 3)
    2. EXP-045 showed per-patient fine-tuning gives 12.6 MAE vs ~16 generic
    3. ERA 3 EXP-371 (fine-tuning) improved only modestly: 27.2 → 26.4

    4. The gap suggests ERA 2 may have been overfitting to patient-specific patterns

    5. Multi-horizon objective dilution

    6. ERA 3 optimizes h30+h60+h120 jointly
    7. ERA 2 optimized single horizon

    8. EXP-383 showed horizon weighting barely helps (+0.2 MAE)

    9. Architecture differences

    10. ERA 2 used simple CNN with fewer parameters
    11. ERA 3 started with heavyweight GroupedEncoder

    12. Simpler models may generalize better for short horizons

    13. Data leakage check — NEGATIVE

    14. EXP-046 (ERA 2): random vs temporal split = 0.2 mg/dL difference
    15. Not the cause — ERA 2 results appear legitimate

    Closing the Gap — Recommendations¶

    Priority Approach Expected Impact Effort
    🔴 HIGH Per-patient fine-tune on EXP-387 champion −3 to −5 mg/dL Low
    🔴 HIGH Single-horizon specialist for 1hr −2 to −4 mg/dL Low
    🟡 MED Apply SWA/cosine to EXP-387 (not base) −1 to −2 mg/dL Low
    🟡 MED CNN architecture (ERA 2 style) + PK channels −2 to −3 mg/dL Medium
    🟢 LOW Larger training set (more patients) −1 to −2 mg/dL High

    Projected best case: 24.4 → ~17 mg/dL (with fine-tuning + specialist) vs ERA 2 target of 12.6 mg/dL. Remaining ~4 mg/dL gap likely requires patient-specific model capacity that cross-patient training inherently lacks.
