    What uploaders do when Nightscout refuses a write¶

    Record

    Record as of 2026-10-25. A point-in-time document; it is not kept up to date.

    Snapshot, 2026-10-25, client sources at the pins in §1; Nightscout side official/dev b29f1bf7 and the draft feat/api3-domain-validation a2e5fe49. Current: the evidence behind the write-validation design's decision that reject stays opt-in until a refusal is visible to the person. Contributor-facing. Current decisions: write-validation design and queue items MOD-WRITE-VALIDATION, MOD-WRITE-VALIDATION-READ; register BF-180.

    Session f4's upload-400 lane asked one question per uploader: if Nightscout answers a write with a 400 (or a 200 that marks one item refused, the D2 reply shape of the write-validation draft), what happens to that record and to the uploader's queue? Three clients were run; the rest were read. Marks: [M] measured, [R] read from source at the pinned commit, not run. Where a figure appears only in the lane's report and not in its raw files, the row says "reported by the lane".

    2. The table¶

    client a 400 on one record or a whole batch a refused slot inside a 200 (D2 shape) other non-2xx mark
    AndroidAPS (API v3, one record per request): treatments, entries, food logged, the record dropped, the cursor moves on n/a any other 4xx, or an exception, blocks that kind's queue with no backoff and no cap; a 500 is retried 3 times within the call (measured; the 100 ms spacing is reported by the lane) 400/422/500 handling [M]; queue consequences [R]
    AndroidAPS: devicestatus, profile store blocks on any non-2xx n/a blocks [R]
    Loop (NightscoutKit) a whole-batch 400, or a reply array shorter than the batch, holds the anchor: the full batch (up to 1000) is resent on every trigger, with no cap the slot without _id is recorded as "NA" and counted as uploaded: lost; its later edits and deletes are skipped needs exactly 200 [R]
    Trio any non-2xx: none of the batch is marked uploaded, the remaining chunks are not sent, and it retries each loop cycle for up to 24 h 2xx marks every item uploaded; the body is not read: lost as 400 [R]
    xDrip+ (Android) entries: the batch stays queued for up to 7 days. Treatments go by PUT one at a time, and a failure marks every queued treatment done, including ones not yet sent n/a (treatments use PUT) — [R]
    xDrip4iOS a refused BG batch blocks the BG cursor for up to 7 days treatments are matched by content; a refused slot without _id stays pending and is resent while it is among the latest 50 — [R]
    nightscout-connect (in process) all items refused: the frame is rejected, retried with backoff, and the entries in that frame are written again the bookmark moves past the refused item: lost — [M]
    oref0 400, 500 and 200 all exit 0: the refused record is lost, and the newest refused record is POSTed again each cycle — as 400 requests [M]; exit codes reported by the lane

    No client reads a refused member or a Warning header. (Read across all seven.)

    3. Measurements¶

    AndroidAPS core/nssdk (dev 3905d352dd, JVM tests UploadRefusalProbeTest added in the lane's scratch copy, mock server):

    server answer what NSAndroidClientImpl did requests
    400 {"status":400,"message":"Temporary Target: targetBottom is above targetTop"} returned a response, code 400, identifier null, the error body kept 1
    422 returned a response, code 422 1
    500 {"status":500,"message":"Mongo Error"} threw UnsuccessfulNightscoutException 4 (1 + 3 retries)

    AndroidAPS string-matches two 400 bodies: "Bad or missing utcOffset field" (it resends with utcOffset: 0) and "cannot be modified by the client" (it marks the record as never uploadable) (core/nssdk/src/commonMain/kotlin/app/aaps/core/nssdk/NSAndroidClientImpl.kt:226, :231 on dev 3905d352dd; :388, :393 for treatments on 7e1d537d49). Any refusal message has to avoid both.

    nightscout-connect 0.1.0 lib/outputs/internal.js, with a fake context whose treatments.create behaves like the write-validation draft under reject (re-run for this note):

    case result bookmark afterwards
    partial: first of two treatments refused (refused slot without _id) resolved treatments 04:30, the refused item's own time: it is past the refused item
    all refused (error, status 400) rejected, "Nightscout internal write failed"; log.error "Internal persistence failed" unset: the frame is retried

    In both runs entries.create had already written the frame's entry before the treatments call.

    oref0 ns-upload.sh against a stub answering 400, 500 and 200 on three paths: the stub logged one POST /api/v1/treatments.json of 2 items for each. That every run exits 0 is reported by the lane.

    5. Implications¶

    1. log is the only validation mode with no effect any client can see. It can be recommended to operators now.
    2. Under reject, a refused record is silently lost for AndroidAPS, Loop, Trio and nightscout-connect, and retried indefinitely by xDrip4iOS; xDrip+ can lose later treatments in the same pass.
    3. A whole-batch 400 wedges Loop and Trio. The D2 partial-batch reply (200, one object per item, refused slots without _id) is the shape that wedges nobody, which is why it was decided.
    4. A default reject needs a refusal the person can see first: clients that read refused (upstream asks), or a Nightscout-side notice when a record is refused. It is not blocked on a date.
    5. Refusal texts must avoid AndroidAPS's two matched strings, and refusals should stay plain 400s: any other 4xx blocks an AndroidAPS queue.

    Upstream notes from the same lane (not Nightscout defects, no register id): xDrip+'s treatment queue drop on a partial failure; AndroidAPS's Temp Basal check reads only durationInMilliseconds, so duration: 0 in minutes passes (TreatmentMapper.kt:172, read); NightscoutKit builds an override's correction range without checking lower ≤ upper; AndroidAPS subscribes to foods where Nightscout's collection is food.
