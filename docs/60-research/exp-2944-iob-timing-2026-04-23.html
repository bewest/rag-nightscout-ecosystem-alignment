    EXP-2944 — IOB-delta mechanism for the recovery gap¶

    Record

    Record as of 2026-04-23. A point-in-time document; it is not kept up to date.

    Date: 2026-04-23 Audience: AID code authors (decisive mechanism finding)

    Scope¶

    Final in-grid mechanism test for the +21 pp Loop_AB_ON vs oref1 recovery gap during sustained-high carb-isolated windows. Tests the true iob column (vs the EXP-2941 SMB-sum proxy which missed basal-insulin contribution) plus net_basal deviation.

    What this is NOT¶

    • Not therapy advice. Both Loop and oref1 designs are engineered trade-offs and patient choice/access dominates AID selection.
    • Not a model of insulin pharmacokinetics — uses controller's reported IOB at 5-min granularity.

    Result — IOB DELTA is the mechanism¶

    Measure Loop_AB_ON oref1 Δ
    Recovery 35.2% 55.4% −20.2%
    iob_start (U at window entry) 2.515 1.973 +0.542
    iob_delta (U over 60-min) +0.588 −0.041 +0.629
    net_basal_mean (U/h deviation) −1.013 −0.698 −0.315
    basal_cut_frac (cells cut) 0.938 0.946 −0.008

    Within-design IOB-start tertile recovery¶

    Design lo IOB mid IOB hi IOB
    Loop_AB_ON 0.357 0.353 0.347
    oref1 0.529 0.562 0.570

    For Loop_AB_ON, starting IOB has zero effect on recovery (0.36 vs 0.35 vs 0.35). For oref1, starting IOB modestly helps (0.53 → 0.57).

    Mechanism interpretation¶

    Loop_AB_ON is still loading IOB during the recovery window (+0.588 U built up over 60 min). Its starting IOB is HIGHER than oref1's (2.51 vs 1.97 U) but that IOB is bound in slow buildup, not yet acting. The insulin Loop "has on board" is mostly future insulin from recently-fired SMBs that haven't peaked yet.

    oref1 is IOB-neutral during the window (−0.041 U). Its dose was front-loaded BEFORE the window and is now actively working. This matches:

    • EXP-2940 time-to-BG-peak: oref1 22.9 min vs Loop_AB_ON 29.7 min (6.8-min faster peak)
    • EXP-2937 sizing-lever: oref1 fires fewer SMBs (2.82 vs 4.31) at smaller per-event size but those SMBs landed earlier
    • EXP-2930 PP front-loading: 2.2× dose density 0–30 min, 6.6× at 30–60 min for oref1

    Basal cut is NOT the lever. Both designs cut basal in 94% of cells; mean cut depths differ slightly but both are aggressive. The basal channel does not distinguish them.

    The within-design tertile pattern confirms the timing diagnosis:

    • For Loop, more starting IOB doesn't help because the IOB curve is still climbing (decline from IOB happens after the 60-min window ends). Recovery is timing-blocked, not IOB-blocked.
    • For oref1, more starting IOB helps because the IOB is at its peak action during the window.

    Three-tier mechanism stack (UPDATED, post EXP-2944)¶

    Tier Lever Evidence Effect
    1. Channel availability SMB-as-correction present EXP-2942 (oref0≈Loop_AB_OFF at 30%) +6 pp
    2. IOB-delivery timing front-load vs back-load EXP-2944 (iob_delta +0.629 U) +20 pp
    3. Dose-sizing logic velocity/absorption-aware EXP-2937 / EXP-2940 (subsumed in tier 2)

    Tier 2 supersedes the prior "dose-sizing logic" framing. The distinguishing feature is WHEN the insulin lands relative to the BG response window, not the dose magnitude per se.

    AID-author actionable lever (REFINED)¶

    The recovery edge comes from delivering insulin EARLIER in the predicted-rise trajectory, so that IOB peaks DURING the recovery window rather than after it. Concrete implications:

    1. Predict-and-fire on rising velocity, not on already-elevated BG. oref1's UAM/dynamic-ISF triggers SMBs while BG is still rising; Loop_AB_ON fires after BG crosses thresholds.
    2. Larger early SMBs, smaller late SMBs. Oref1 fires fewer SMBs total but front-loads aggressively.
    3. Time-to-peak is the metric. Aim for IOB peak within 20–25 min of the predicted high crossing, not 35–45 min.

    Methodological correction to EXP-2941¶

    EXP-2941 tested prior_smb_3h as an IOB proxy and found distributions identical (Loop 2.749 vs oref1 3.072 U). This proxy missed the basal-insulin component AND misframed the question: starting IOB is not the gap; iob_delta during the window is. Both designs have similar pre-window IOB; they differ in whether that IOB is still climbing (Loop) or peaking (oref1).

    The EXP-2941 selection-bias flag was therefore a measurement artifact. Combined with EXP-2942 (cross-cohort match) and EXP-2943 (η²=0.640), the selection-bias hypothesis is now closed.

    Carry-forward invariants¶

    • IOB timing > IOB magnitude for sustained-high recovery. Two designs with equal starting IOB can have +20 pp recovery difference based on timing.
    • In-grid mechanism searches must use the iob column directly, not SMB-sum proxies. Basal contribution is non-trivial.
    • basal_cut_frac is design-equivalent at sustained-high entries (94% across Loop_AB_ON and oref1). Basal posture is not a correction-loop differentiator.

    Three-tier mechanism map (final, post-2944)¶

    Window Loop offence oref1 offence
    PP 0-60min AB_ON only, late-fire UAM front-load
    PP 60-240min basal cut + smaller SMBs continued cadence
    Sustained-high entry back-loaded IOB (+0.588 U during window) front-loaded IOB (peaking)
