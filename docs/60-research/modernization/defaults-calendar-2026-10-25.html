    cgm-remote-monitor: operator-visible defaults, flags and behaviour changes, and a proposed calendar¶

    Record

    Record as of 2026-10-25. A point-in-time document; it is not kept up to date.

    DRAFT proposal, 2026-10-25, for the maintainers and the community. Read-only inventory against official/master = v15.0.9 c6a0d8ed, official/dev b29f1bf7 and the tips of cuts 3, 4 and 5 (§0). The maintainer's word on the calendar (2026-10-25): it stays a draft, and the offsets are decided by maintainers and the community together; nothing in §3 is scheduled. BF-72 and BF-161 appear by mechanism only, because both are live on 15.0.9. Contributor-facing. Current facts: backfix register; 16.0.0 release records; versioning policy §5.7.

    3. Proposed calendar¶

    3.1 The rule¶

    Each change carries a not-before date. The announce step names that date (release notes, and an env.notifies admin notice when the site is actually affected), and the change lands in the first release of the required class cut on or after it. A strictness change becomes a default only in a major; announce and opt-in may ship in a minor; corrections and refactors ship in any release once verified. Nothing refuses to start (BF-67/86 decision); a refusal shows a page or a banner that names the fix.

    3.2 Slots¶

    T = the 16.0.0 release date (not yet set). Offsets are proposals for maintainers and the community to decide.

    slot release not before class

    S1 16.1.0 T + 8 weeks minor
    S2 16.2.0 T + 16 weeks minor
    S3 17.0.0 T + 6 months major
    S4 18.0.0 T + 12 months major
    any patch or minor when verified refactors and corrections
