    Access-loss log: when open diabetes software lost, or never had, access¶

    Record

    Record as of 2026-10-17. A point-in-time document; it is not kept up to date.

    For the Nightscout Foundation board, the maintainers of the ecosystem projects, and the community. Research note, snapshot of 2026-10-17. Dated events in which open-source diabetes software lost, or never got, access to a device or a vendor cloud, and when access came back. Every row cites a commit, issue, pull request or URL. Used by why organise §3. A shared intake (partner readiness §6) would carry this forward with counts of the people each event reached, which no source here records.

    All rows read 2026-10-17 unless marked otherwise. "Lag" is measured from the vendor's first launch or clearance named in the row to the first public open-software commit or release named in the row. Mechanism level only: no header values, endpoints, keys or request formats are recorded here.

    Abbreviations: NS = Nightscout; LLU = Abbott LibreLinkUp (follower app and its cloud); nsc = nightscout/nightscout-connect; nslu = timoschlueter/nightscout-librelink-up; mmc = nightscout/minimed-connect-to-nightscout; s2nb = nightscout/share2nightscout-bridge; tcs = jwoglom/tconnectsync.

    2. Gaps¶

    • Medtronic Paradigm model/firmware limits for open control, 640G and 780G EU launch dates: not sourced here (maintainer's account).
    • Dexcom G7 US commercial launch day (early 2023): not found in a primary source; only the 2022-12-08 clearance.
    • Libre 2 CE date, Libre 3 CE month (sources say September or October 2020), Libre 3 FDA month, and Juggluco's first Libre 3 release before its public repo (2023-01-29).
    • Omnipod 5: whether AAPS or any other project had public Omnipod 5 code before 2026-02-22; OmnipodKit commit 37765301 (2025-03-20) mentions an "O5 selection option" and was not examined further.
    • Tandem t:connect shutdown date; Sugarmate: no shutdown of the app itself found (still releasing, 4.2.0 on 2026-03-02).
    • Dexcom ONE/ONE+/Stelo, Libre 2 US vs EU firmware, Dexcom G6 "Firefly" transmitter changes (xDrip+ commits from 2021-05-16 only), Roche Accu-Chek SmartGuide pairing: not dated or sourced in this pass.
    • The FDA 2019 safety communication was read through press coverage, not the FDA page.
    • Maintainer's belief that LLU added or is about to add measures that break connectors: the encrypted 5.x API (April 2026) is confirmed; a date for 4.x being switched off is not.

    3. Counts¶

    37 rows (12 device, 25 cloud). Excluding the Paradigm baseline row, the Sugarmate Alexa/macOS sunset and the undiagnosed 2026-10-17 report, 34 events remain: 11 device, 23 cloud.

    Device (11). Generation not openly accessible for control: 3 (530G; 600 series, read-only; 770G/780G, proof of concept only). Access removed: 3 (2019 Paradigm/508 recall; 2019 and 2021/2023 Abbott DMCA notices).     Cloud (23): LibreLinkUp 8, Tandem 6, CareLink 4, Dexcom Share 3, Dexcom-to-Sugarmate 1, Glooko 1. Time to a fix in at least one connector: 7 days or less for 11 (including LibreLinkUp October 2025, fixed in nightscout-librelink-up before the deadline but only 11 months later in nightscout-connect); 15 days for 1 (CareLink care partner); about 5 weeks for 1 (Sugarmate, US); about 7 weeks for 1 (Share 2020); 2.8 months for 1 (Share 2026 in nightscout-connect); 14.7 months for 1 (Glooko in nightscout-connect); not dated for 1 (t:connect to Tandem Source). Not yet a loss: 1 (LibreLinkUp 5.x encrypted API; the 4.x API was still working in the sources read).
