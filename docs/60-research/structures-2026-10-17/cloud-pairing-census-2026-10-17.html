    Manufacturer accounts and online steps at setup: public-document census¶

    Record

    Record as of 2026-10-17. A point-in-time document; it is not kept up to date.

    For the Nightscout Foundation board, the maintainers of the ecosystem projects, and the community. Research note, snapshot of 2026-10-17. What each manufacturer's own system requires at setup and pairing, as its public documents state (manuals, instructions for use, support pages, app-store listings). Nothing here comes from reverse-engineering or open-source code; mechanism level only. Rows marked "(snippet)" were read from a search-engine summary of a page that could not be fetched, and need a re-read before they are quoted. Used by why organise §4 and the cloud column of tools/structure-diagrams/access/devices.yaml.

    How to read this¶

    • Account to set up: does the manufacturer's app or controller require signing in to a manufacturer account before the device can be used? Quoted phrase is the manufacturer's.
    • Online step / cloud credential: does a public document say the device or app needs an internet connection during setup or pairing, or that a credential is issued by the manufacturer's servers? "not stated" means no public document read says so either way.
    • Offline after setup: does a public document say the device keeps working without internet? Statements that only cloud features (upload, follow) need internet are recorded as "cloud features only", which implies but does not state offline operation.
    • Launch year: approximate, from manufacturer press/trade coverage; marked "approx." and not verified row by row.
    • Where the only source was a search-engine snippet of a manufacturer page that could not be fetched directly (403/binary PDF), the row says "(snippet)". Treat those as needing a re-read.

    What the public documents show overall¶

    1. Every phone app for a current CGM line read here (Dexcom, Abbott, Eversense, Roche, Sibionics) is documented as requiring a manufacturer account at first setup. Dedicated receivers/readers are documented without one (Libre reader, Dexcom receiver).
    2. Only three lines have a public statement that the setup or pairing step itself needs the internet: Dexcom ONE+ ("secure internet access during setup"), Eversense 365 ("internet connection ... during the pairing process") and CamAPS FX ("during the download and sign-up process"). Omnipod 5's Omnipod ID sign-in on the controller is an online first-use step by description.
    3. One line publicly documents app-delivered certificates: the iLet app "loads certificates" to enable G7. No other manufacturer document read here describes a cloud-issued credential.
    4. Offline operation after setup is almost never stated positively. Pumps are documented as delivering independently of the app/PDM (t:slim X2, DASH, Medtrum); CGM apps are documented only as needing internet for cloud features. Eversense is the one documented negative: logged out, the app shows no glucose data.
